# Protocol — Study v3: decisive power study for MAP-Elites vs fixed-objective GA

**Registered before the run.**

## Why this study and why this scope

A power analysis on study v2's paired differences (computed before this study was
run) gives:

| method | observed paired mean | paired sd | n for 80% power (α=0.05, 2-sided) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `novelty_search` | +0.331 | 2.115 | **321** |
| `map_elites` | +0.790 | 1.583 | **31.5** |

`novelty_search` cannot be resolved at any feasible sample size on this task, so it
is **out of scope** here and no claim about it is made. `map_elites` is feasible, so
study v3 is deliberately scoped to the one comparison that can be decided.

This is a *scoping decision made on power grounds before running*, not after seeing
a favourable result. Dropping a method is stated here so it cannot be read as
selective reporting.

## Hypothesis (restricted)

**H1-ME.** Under an equal interaction budget, MAP-Elites yields higher held-out
reward than fixed-objective evolutionary optimization on this task.

H0: the mean paired difference is zero.

## Design (fixed)

* **Task and budget:** identical to the pilot and studies 1/v2 (10×10 egocentric
  foraging, 500,000 interactions/method/seed). No re-tuning.
* **Seeds:** `[21 … 60]` — 40 seeds, **disjoint** from study 1 (1–10) and study v2
  (11–20). n = 40 exceeds the computed requirement of 31.5 and gives a minimum
  detectable paired effect of ≈ **0.70** at 80% power.
* **Methods:** `map_elites` and `fixed_objective_ga`, plus `random` and
  `heuristic` controls. `novelty_search` and `reinforce` are excluded (see above).
* **Held-out seeds:** `[101, 202, 303, 404]`, never used for tuning.

## Registered primary analysis (fixed)

Per seed `s`: `d_s = held_out(map_elites, s) − held_out(fixed_objective_ga, s)`.

1. **Paired bootstrap 95% percentile CI** on `mean(d)`, 10,000 resamples,
   RNG seed **20261010** (distinct from studies 1 and v2).
2. **Wilcoxon signed-rank**, two-sided, α = 0.05.
3. **Decision rule:** H1-ME *supported* if the point estimate is positive and the CI
   excludes 0; *falsified* if the CI excludes 0 negative; otherwise **inconclusive**,
   reported with the minimum detectable effect so the null is *bounded* rather than
   merely unproven.

## Reporting rules

* A CI spanning zero at n=40 is reported as: "no MAP-Elites advantage larger than
  ≈0.70 on this task", not as a near-miss.
* Secondary: the unpaired Mann–Whitney U, for comparability with studies 1/v2.
* All numbers generated by `scripts/analyze.py --design paired`.
